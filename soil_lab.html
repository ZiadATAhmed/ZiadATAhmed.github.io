🔬 Soil Detectives: Testing Soil Samples

Grade 7 · NGSS MS-LS2-3 Digital Lab

Your mission

The city wants to know which places around town have healthy soil and which places need help before anything new gets planted. Your team will collect soil from 5 different sites, bring the samples back to the lab, and test each one for organic matter.

🍂 Healthy soil contains organic matter: the remains of once-living plants and animals. Organic matter contains carbon.
🧪 We can test for organic matter using hydrogen peroxide. When peroxide is added to soil with lots of organic matter, it reacts with the carbon and makes gas bubbles (carbon dioxide).
⏱️ More organic matter = more bubbling for a longer time. You will time how long each sample bubbles.

Your lab group (4 people)

What you'll do

  1. Suit up with lab safety gear.
  2. Visit 5 sites, dig up soil, and record what you observe.
  3. In the lab: pour soil into a cup, add hydrogen peroxide, and time the bubbles.
  4. Mark the class timeline with an X.
  5. Analyze your data and make a claim.

🧪 Lab bench: pick a sample to test

Each bag gets its own clean cup. Test all 5 samples.

  1. Predict how long it will bubble.
  2. Pour soil in, just enough to cover the bottom.
  3. Start the stopwatch.
  4. Slowly pour hydrogen peroxide to moisten the soil.
  5. Stop the stopwatch when the bubbles stop.
