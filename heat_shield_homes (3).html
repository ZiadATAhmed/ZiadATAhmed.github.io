Heat Shield Homes

Your mission: design a home with a living room, bedroom, bathroom, and kitchen that stays comfortable all year while using as little electricity as possible for heating and cooling.

The big ideaHeat always moves from hotter places to colder places. Conductors let heat pass through easily. Insulators slow heat down.
  1. Build walls on the grid, or use the 🏷️ Rooms tool to drop in a ready-made room. You need a living room, bedroom, bathroom, and kitchen.
  2. Add windows and doors. Every room needs a door, and the house needs a door to the outside.
  3. Pick materials for the walls, windows, doors, roof, and floor. Watch your building budget.
  4. Furnish each room with the equipment it needs. Add wallpaper, and paint your own pixel art for the walls.
  5. Run the 24-hour test to see how warm or cold each room gets with the heater and AC turned off.
  6. Run the Year test to see how much electricity your house uses through all four seasons.
  7. Add up to 4 houses to compare them side by side.

Scientist tip: Use the ⧉ button to copy a house, then change only one thing. Now you can see exactly what that one change does.

Controls: drag with the 👁️ Look tool (or right-drag) to spin the view. Scroll to zoom. Ctrl+Z undoes. R turns furniture.
