Lab rules: memorize these

You will not see these rules again.

  • 1. If the question number is a ★, end your answer with the word red. Example: Question ★ has the answer 12. You type: 12 red
  • 2. If the question number is a ▲, start your answer with the word up. Example: Question ▲ has the answer 12. You type: up 12
  • 3. On question 20, the last one, end your answer with the word done.

Other tips: type your answer and press Enter. You can't go back to a question, so take your time.
