Proton to Iron

You are a single proton floating in the hot plasma of a star. Grab particles to grow into heavier and heavier atoms. Reach iron, then dive into the star's core and see where your atom ends up.

  • Move: WASD or arrow keys. On a tablet, drag on the left side of the screen.
  • Laser: hold the mouse button or Space and aim with the mouse. On a tablet, touch and hold on the right side.
  • Protons and electrons are charged, so your laser pulls them in. The more negative you are, the harder you pull protons. The more positive you are, the harder you pull electrons. Like charges push each other away.
  • Neutrons have no charge, so the laser can't grab them. Chase one down and keep your nucleus on it until the ring fills.
  • Bond quests: become the right atom with the right number of valence electrons, then press Q to summon a partner atom and earn an item.
  • E releases an electron. R releases a proton, which turns you back into the element before. T opens the periodic table.

Real stars mostly build heavy atoms by smashing whole nuclei together, like helium into carbon. In this game you build one particle at a time so you can see every step.
